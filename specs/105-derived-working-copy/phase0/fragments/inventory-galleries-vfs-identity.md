## Verdict / evidence vocabulary

- **Decision evidence — SOURCE**: a decision-store record exists *before* the write and the write is its apply/effect. Decision id + module named.
- **Decision evidence — LOG-ONLY**: no decision record sources the write; at most a decision-*log* entry is appended (`recordEditorStep` / `recordGalleryDecisions` / `recordStartContribution`, all in `decisions/`, writing to `decisionLogStore`, not `decisionStore`).
- **Decision evidence — BRIDGE**: the working copy (WC) is the edit-time source of truth; a decision record is *snapshotted from* the WC at step completion only ("record-from-working-copy", ruling D-090-31). The decision copies the WC; the WC was never derived from the decision.
- **Decision evidence — NONE**: no decision record and no log entry names this state.
- **Verdict — HAS-DECISION-SOURCE**: evidence is SOURCE.
- **Verdict — BRIDGE-ONLY**: evidence is BRIDGE or LOG-ONLY — a decision/log record exists downstream, but deleting the WC write loses the state; the decision cannot re-derive it at edit time.
- **Verdict — NO-DECISION-SOURCE**: evidence is NONE.

Store shorthand: WC = `stores/workingCopyStore.ts` (`useWorkingCopyStore`); AS = `stores/surveyAnswerStore.ts` (`useSurveyAnswerStore`); DS = `stores/decisionStore.ts` (`useDecisionStore`).
